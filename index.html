About

I build the unglamorous layer that turns a generative-AI demo into a system an enterprise can actually depend on.

Since 2022 I have worked across the full path from prototype to production: agentic pipelines that map and restructure messy enterprise data, the FastAPI services that expose them, the CI/CD that ships them, and the load tests that prove they hold. Most of that work has been for large CPG and enterprise clients, where the measure of success is not a benchmark score but whether an analyst's week gets shorter.

My background is quantitative before it was computational — a chemical engineering degree from IIT Patna and a published stochastic-optimisation model. From September 2026 I am in London for an MSc in AI and Medical Imaging at UCL, pointing that systems experience at clinical problems.
